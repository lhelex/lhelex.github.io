Roman Empire

Won Grand Champion in SIKAPTala 2026 Game Jam (National Computer Science and Information Technology Competition). A visual novel puzzle game that was developed as an entry for the SIKAPTala 2026 Game Jam with the theme "We Don't See Things as They Are." I created the game's background assets for the 48 hour project that secured 1st place out of 28 competing teams.

Check it out here!

Codename: Vista

Currently a work in progress project for our thesis! A classic 3D side scrolling turn-based combat experience, where it integrates aspects of environmental storytelling into its gameplay mechanics, narrative structure, and visual design, to understand the representation of the “Art vs. AI” within the gaming industry.

Red Tide

Made by me and my groupmate during our Software Engineering course! Red Tide is a 2D beat-'em-up game that incorporates a single button for all of the weapon combos built using GameMaker Studio. The game aims to be a tight, combat-focused beat-‘em-up with a functional collision and enemy systems and where the players engage in melee combat against a wave of enemies.

Check it out here!
